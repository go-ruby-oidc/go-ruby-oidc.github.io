go-ruby-oidc is a pure-Go (no cgo) OpenID Connect library — provider discovery, JWKS fetch / cache / key-selection, ID-token signature-and-claim verification, the Authorization-Code + PKCE login flow, and UserInfo — mirroring the surface of Ruby's openid_connect gem where it maps cleanly onto deterministic protocol logic, without any Ruby runtime. It builds on its siblings — the OAuth2 authorization-URL / token-request construction comes from go-ruby-oauth2 and the ID-token JWS parse / verify from go-ruby-jwt — and adds the OIDC-specific pieces: discovery, JWKS→key selection, and the ID-token claim validation. Every network round-trip is an injectable Doer seam, so the core never opens a socket; it is the OIDC layer bound into go-embedded-ruby by rbgo, 100% coverage — every rejection branch — CI green across 6 arches including the big-endian lane.
